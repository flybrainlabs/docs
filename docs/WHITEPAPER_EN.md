# flybrainlabs: Verifiable computation and finite-budget settlement V2

Version: 2026-10-11. Brain Simulation Mining is a verifiable computation network
based on neural simulation. Make computation produce verifiable, reusable results.

## Computation and scientific scope

MaleCNS v1.0 supplies anatomy, not newly discovered structural connections. The
registered integer point LIF model has 165122 neurons and 25563197 directed neuron
pairs, not that many raw biological synapses. Leak, accumulation, threshold,
reset, refractory time and connection propagation use frozen numerical rules.
256 steps of 1 ms define 256 ms of model time; execution time is measured separately.
Current, membrane state and stimulus cost are model units, not calibrated mV, pA or joules.

A/B share model, zero initial state and seed; B adds the stimulus. G net spikes,
positive cells, O absolute changes, selectivity and cost use windows relative to
stimulus end. R counts required backgrounds, not institutions or biological confidence.
The frozen control background cannot trigger a first spike. Transmitter-sign
mapping is an assumption. This point model does not solve dendritic compartments;
O32 constrains a local set, not whole-brain side effects.

Task pass-rate and cost calibration, full protocol replay, independent implementation
reproduction and external observation support have distinct evidence requirements.
Without reviewed observations, no biological accuracy or generalization is asserted.

## Goal competition and verification

Target V2 freezes each round's model, input domain, windows, backgrounds and deadlines.
The product group has 4×3×1×6=72 legal combinations. Different readout groups share
one base model. Renaming, wallets and repeated solved goals do not create new supply.
When supply is exhausted, the miner waits for new calibrated tasks.

Commit, private upload and reveal precede complete replay. The earliest still-eligible
verified authority receipt sequence wins; client clocks, upload speed and vote timing
do not select the winner. One current operator controls three keys with a 2-of-3
policy. This is not independent institutions or permissionless consensus. Full replay
does not prove a hardware workload, minimum energy consumption or free verification.
Strictly equivalent optimized implementations remain valid. Partial failures preserve
actual completed coverage. Cached trajectories and new readouts are not newly simulated work.

## Scientific identity and publication

SimulationId uses `FlyPoW/science-simulation-v1/v2\0` plus canonical JSON for source,
graph/model/parameter roots, numerical rules, initial state, canonical input, actual
seed, time step and range. It excludes miner, round, readout, filename and title.
ReadoutId binds sorted unique simulations, G/O roots, windows, evaluator and rules.
ContributionId binds actor, contribution type, scientific reference, task, budget and
authorization. A separate AcceptanceId binds judgement, policy, evidence and issuer.
Original V1/V2 identities, signatures, receipts and scientific bytes remain unchanged.

Only explicitly published packages enter the public result API. Private candidates,
salts, paths, secrets and requests remain private. The public calibration case has
new complete native replay and independent event evaluation; it remains a calibration
case, not a current-round win or a biological discovery. Downloads have allowlists,
expiry signatures, MIME, sizes, SHA-256 and resumable ranges. Reproduction is explicit;
browsing never starts workers or uploads local history.

## EconomicPolicyV2

One policy defines a cap of 311833243 FBW at 8 decimals: 31183324300000000 atoms.
Pools are 224519936 miners, 56129984 verification and 31183323 community/ecosystem,
approximately 72%, 18%, 10%, with exact integer conservation. Checked integers and
canonical decimal strings are mandatory. The cap is not circulating supply or a
biological price. A singleton program account binds the unique mint and three vaults.
One atomic issuance marks issued, mints exact amounts and revokes mint authority;
CPI failure rolls back. Nonzero supply, wrong authority, retries and another campaign
cannot create a second cap. An existing asset follows a separate supply/vault adoption path.
Upgrade authority and admitted acceptance-key trust remain explicit.

Continuous tasks use finite funded stages. Policy, source, task quotas, deadlines,
beneficiaries, acceptance and residual-fund rules freeze before participation.
Fixed atom allocations are preferred; cumulative quotas use
`floor((k+1)*P/M)-floor(k*P/M)` in atoms, distinct from historical V1 whole-token rounding.
New goals use new budgets and never postpone old confirmed rights. Unbudgeted tasks
promise no reward. Discovery business slots, published coverage units, authorized
verification orders (including correct rejection), and new-readout work are separate.
Threads, client throughput, uptime, failures or wallet changes create no economic right.

## Three ledgers and finality

Scientific records, contribution rights and asset funds have stable linked IDs.
Reservations, confirmed liability, in-flight payments and final payments are disjoint
atomic transitions. An append-only event chain and durable outbox complement business
uniqueness. Settlement rechecks asset, pool, beneficiary, quota and permanent payment
consumption. Unknown RPC outcomes stay locked until the original transaction and
consumption record are reconciled. Only definitive expired non-execution unlocks retry.
Partial/concurrent claims cannot exceed rights. Receipt requires configured `finalized`
confirmation, not a winner field or submitted transaction.

Cancellation does not revoke accepted unconditional rights. Only unencumbered residue
can be released or refunded to its frozen recipient. There is no arbitrary administrator
sweep of unclaimed rights. Quotes disclose services, verification, storage, transfer,
chain fees, account principal, deposits and advances with expiry and caps. The historical
1024-byte receipt is one account, not total operating cost. New deposits and unused
advances are refundable under frozen rules; any development-fund charge must be explicit.
Assets are accounted separately. Missing prices remain unknown. Token issuance is not
external income, deposits are not profit and prepaid cash is not recognized revenue.
A sponsorship must not be counted twice as task revenue and period funding.

## Services and operations

Bounded stimulus search, response comparison, public sponsorship and result delivery
have a real persistent request intake, private receipt query, signed operator follow-up
and expiring quote workflow. Saved requests are not purchases or payments. Arbitrary
customer text is not executable code. Restricted requests require manual review; public
miner input is not confidential execution. Request fields are retained at most 365 days
then redacted; disclosed audit and contractual records are retained separately.
