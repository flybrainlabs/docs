## Wallet Mining V3 on Devnet

Wallet Standard UI, origin-bound sign-in, finite grants, durable financial intents, atomic Result creation/payment and history are implemented. Public Devnet acceptance completed two distinct full-model winners under one grant, paying **one FBW each**.

The initial two-unit acceptance plan is complete. A separate **task-supply-20261013-v1** stage now funds two unused, prospectively calibrated full-model targets: **one FBW each, two FBW total**. Scientific plan revision 3 opened round 32, with the next goal queued. The mining page shows live availability; only the corresponding frozen unit can pay its verified winner. Model preparation, wallet history and applicable unused-principal withdrawal remain usable after a finite stage completes.

The first dedicated Result was closed after archive certification; the second remains readable. Permanent payment evidence prevents a closed Result from causing a second payment or a second rent debit. See [FBW Devnet reference](/docs/devnet/) for the public receipts.

## Marketplace and future network features

The proposed catalog, draft price templates, migrations and read-only catalog API are implemented. Host-tested financial primitives enforce finite allowances, accounting and claims. Native service workers, real Ridge training/export, service SBF/SPL integration, finite paid API usage, settlement/refunds and historical-claim flows remain incomplete.

Installable open validators, non-fixed-domain admission, staking and independent multi-operator participation are future work. Mainnet, formal production issuance and customer payment routes remain inactive. Read the [Marketplace roadmap](/docs/marketplace/) and [Economic model](/docs/economics/) for the prerequisites.

## How to interpret status

A numerical replay confirms the declared model computation. A browser test confirms its tested interaction or layout. A finalized chain receipt confirms that financial action. Physical-device and external-science claims require their respective evidence. Each guide keeps these scopes separate so you can assess what an outcome actually establishes.


## Community participation

Social provides signed-in wallet challenges, X public evidence verification, durable manual review, community points and a real leaderboard. Published budgets are preparation scope: no token transfer or immediate claim is enabled. Official OAuth and actual extension/human X confirmation require separate external acceptance. See the [community guide](/docs/social/).
